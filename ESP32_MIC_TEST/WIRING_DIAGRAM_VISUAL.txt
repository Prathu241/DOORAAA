STEP-BY-STEP WIRING (Color-Coded)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Wire 1: RED (3.3V Power)
────────────────────────────────────────
  ESP32-S3 [3V3] --RED--> Breadboard [+] rail
                            ↓
                      INMP441 VCC (pin 6)
  
  Action: Plug RED into ESP32 3V3, route to top-left [+] rail,
          Connect INMP441 VCC into same rail

  Safety: Check - no shorts, RED not touching BLACK


Wire 2 & 3: BLACK (Ground - 2 wires!)
────────────────────────────────────────
  Wire 2a:
  ESP32-S3 [GND] --BLACK--> Breadboard [-] rail
                              ↓
                        INMP441 GND (pin 1)
  
  Wire 2b:
  [same GND rail] --BLACK--> INMP441 L/R (pin 2)
  
  Action: Plug BLACK #1 into ESP32 GND, route to bottom-left [-] rail
          From [-] rail, run separate BLACK #2 to INMP441 GND (pin 1)
          From [-] rail, run separate BLACK #3 to INMP441 L/R (pin 2)
  
  Safety: Check - L/R is BOTH connected to GND (CRITICAL for mono mode!)


Wire 4: YELLOW (GPIO 4 → SCK Bit Clock)
────────────────────────────────────────
  ESP32-S3 [GPIO 4] --YELLOW--> INMP441 SCK (pin 3)
  
  Action: Plug YELLOW into GPIO 4 (left side, labeled "4")
          Route directly to INMP441 SCK (pin 3)


Wire 5: GREEN (GPIO 5 → WS Word Select)
────────────────────────────────────────
  ESP32-S3 [GPIO 5] --GREEN--> INMP441 WS (pin 5)
  
  Action: Plug GREEN into GPIO 5 (left side, labeled "5")
          Route directly to INMP441 WS (pin 5)


Wire 6: BLUE (GPIO 6 → SD Data In)
────────────────────────────────────────
  ESP32-S3 [GPIO 6] --BLUE--> INMP441 SD (pin 4)
  
  Action: Plug BLUE into GPIO 6 (left side, labeled "6")
          Route directly to INMP441 SD (pin 4)

WHAT EACH METRIC MEANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

rms       = Root Mean Square (overall volume) - higher = louder
peak      = Loudest single sample - shows sudden spikes
min       = Most negative value - shows bass/depth
max       = Most positive value - shows peaks


✓ MIC WORKING when:
  - Values change from ~40-50 (silence) → 200-300+ (speaking)
  - Peak shows clear spikes when clapping
  - Patterns are consistent across measurements

✗ MIC NOT WORKING when:
  - All zeros all the time
  - Values stuck at same number
  - Errors in Serial Monitor
