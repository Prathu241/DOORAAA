║                    WIRING - SIMPLE STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE                        ║
║                     (Follow these steps in exact order)                       ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝


BEFORE YOU START:
✓ Unplug ESP32-S3 from USB (NO POWER while wiring)
✓ Gather all 6 wires (RED, 2× BLACK, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE)
✓ Have your INMP441 microphone module ready
✓ Breadboard on flat surface


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: IDENTIFY YOUR PINS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Your INMP441 Module (circular, flat side shows text):

Look at the FLAT SIDE with text printed on it:

        GND ---- VCC
        L/R ---- WS
        SCK ---- SD

Numbering (top-left clockwise):
  1. GND   (top-left)
  2. L/R   (middle-left)
  3. SCK   (bottom-left)
  4. SD    (bottom-right)
  5. WS    (middle-right)
  6. VCC   (top-right)

⚠️ CRITICAL: L/R pin (2) MUST go to GND for MONO mode!


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: POWER CONNECTIONS (RED & BLACK WIRES)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RED WIRE (3.3V Power):
────────────────────────

1. Find ESP32-S3 3.3V pin
   - Look at TOP side of ESP32-S3
   - Left side near the USB port
   - Label shows: 3V3 or 3.3V

2. Plug RED wire into 3.3V pin

3. Route RED wire to breadboard TOP-LEFT [+] rail

4. Plug INMP441 VCC (pin 6) into same [+] rail

ACTION: RED wire connects ESP32 3.3V → [+] rail → INMP441 pin 6


BLACK WIRE #1 (Ground):
──────────────────────

1. Find ESP32-S3 GND pin
   - Multiple GND pins available
   - Pick ANY one (usually on sides or bottom)

2. Plug BLACK wire into GND pin

3. Route BLACK wire to breadboard BOTTOM-LEFT [-] rail

4. Plug INMP441 GND (pin 1) into [-] rail

ACTION: BLACK wire #1 connects ESP32 GND → [-] rail → INMP441 pin 1


BLACK WIRE #2 (Ground for Mono):
──────────────────────────────

1. From same [-] rail (where you plugged BLACK wire #1)

2. Plug INMP441 L/R (pin 2) into [-] rail

⚠️ BOTH pin 1 AND pin 2 go to GND (same rail)
   This sets INMP441 to MONO mode

ACTION: BLACK wire #2 (same rail) → INMP441 pin 2


SAFETY CHECK AT THIS POINT:
☐ RED wire NOT touching BLACK wire
☐ RED wire solid connection to 3.3V and [+] rail
☐ BLACK wires solid in GND and [-] rail
☐ INMP441 VCC (pin 6) in [+] rail
☐ INMP441 GND (pin 1) in [-] rail
☐ INMP441 L/R (pin 2) in [-] rail (same as GND)


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: SIGNAL CONNECTIONS (YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE WIRES)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YELLOW WIRE (GPIO 4 → SCK Clock):
──────────────────────────────────

1. Find ESP32-S3 GPIO 4 pin
   - LEFT side of ESP32-S3
   - Pin labeled: 4 or GPIO4

2. Plug YELLOW wire into GPIO 4

3. Route YELLOW wire to breadboard MIDDLE area

4. Plug INMP441 SCK (pin 3) into breadboard

ACTION: YELLOW wire: GPIO 4 → INMP441 pin 3


GREEN WIRE (GPIO 5 → WS Word Select):
──────────────────────────────────────

1. Find ESP32-S3 GPIO 5 pin
   - LEFT side of ESP32-S3, right below GPIO 4
   - Pin labeled: 5 or GPIO5

2. Plug GREEN wire into GPIO 5

3. Route GREEN wire to breadboard

4. Plug INMP441 WS (pin 5) into breadboard

ACTION: GREEN wire: GPIO 5 → INMP441 pin 5


BLUE WIRE (GPIO 6 → SD Data):
──────────────────────────────

1. Find ESP32-S3 GPIO 6 pin
   - LEFT side of ESP32-S3, right below GPIO 5
   - Pin labeled: 6 or GPIO6

2. Plug BLUE wire into GPIO 6

3. Route BLUE wire to breadboard

4. Plug INMP441 SD (pin 4) into breadboard

ACTION: BLUE wire: GPIO 6 → INMP441 pin 4


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: FINAL VERIFICATION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Check all 6 connections:

┌─ POWER (3 wires)
│  ☐ RED:     ESP32 3.3V  → [+] rail → INMP441 VCC (pin 6)
│  ☐ BLACK #1: ESP32 GND  → [-] rail → INMP441 GND (pin 1)
│  ☐ BLACK #2: ESP32 GND  → [-] rail → INMP441 L/R (pin 2)
│
├─ SIGNALS (3 wires)
│  ☐ YELLOW: ESP32 GPIO 4 → INMP441 SCK (pin 3)
│  ☐ GREEN:  ESP32 GPIO 5 → INMP441 WS  (pin 5)
│  ☐ BLUE:   ESP32 GPIO 6 → INMP441 SD  (pin 4)
│
├─ SAFETY
│  ☐ No loose connections (all wires firmly seated)
│  ☐ No bent pins on INMP441
│  ☐ RED and BLACK do not touch
│  ☐ Breadboard clean and dry
│
└─ READY: All checks pass


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: PLUG IN USB
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Verify all wiring is correct (use checklist above)

2. Connect USB cable to ESP32-S3

3. Connect USB to your computer

4. ESP32 should power on (LED might blink)

⚠️ If USB is plugged in BEFORE wiring is complete:
   Risk of shorts - ALWAYS wire first, USB last!


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After USB plugged in:

✓ ESP32-S3 LED might blink or light up
✓ No smoke or burning smell
✓ INMP441 module might have a small LED (optional)
✓ Everything should be cool to touch

If you see:
✗ Smoke → UNPLUG IMMEDIATELY, check for shorts
✗ Hot components → UNPLUG, review wiring
✗ Nothing works → Check USB cable, try different port

NEXT STEP: FLASH FIRMWARE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Once wiring is complete and verified:

1. Open Arduino IDE
2. Copy mic_test.ino code into new sketch
3. Select Board: ESP32-S3 Dev Module
4. Select Port: COMx (your ESP32's port)
5. Click Upload

See: Arduino IDE setup instructions in README_MIC_TEST.md


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ WRONG: L/R pin floating (not connected)
   ✓ RIGHT: L/R pin connected to GND (same as pin 1)

❌ WRONG: GPIO pins 4, 5, 6 confused
   ✓ RIGHT: GPIO 4→SCK, GPIO 5→WS, GPIO 6→SD

❌ WRONG: Using different GND connections for L/R
   ✓ RIGHT: Both L/R and GND use same breadboard rail

❌ WRONG: Plugging USB before wiring
   ✓ RIGHT: Wire everything first, USB last

❌ WRONG: Loose connections in breadboard
   ✓ RIGHT: Push wires firmly into holes until seated
